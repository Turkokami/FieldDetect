The five exam domains

The ACE exam measures your ability to deploy, operate, and secure solutions on Google Cloud. Google lists these five areas of skills measured.

1

Setting up a cloud environment

Projects, billing accounts, budgets, the Cloud SDK/CLI, Cloud Shell, and the resource hierarchy (org → folder → project).

2

Planning & configuring

Choosing compute, storage, and network options; estimating cost with the Pricing Calculator; planning for scale.

3

Deploying & implementing

Deploying Compute Engine, GKE, Cloud Run, App Engine, Cloud Functions, storage, and networking — console and gcloud.

4

Ensuring successful operation

Cloud Monitoring, Logging, alerts, snapshots, images, quotas, and day-2 management of running resources.

5

Configuring access & security

IAM roles, service accounts, least privilege, audit logs, org policies, KMS/CMEK, and Secret Manager.
